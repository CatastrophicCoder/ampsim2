### Magnitude error

The error is largest towards 20 kHz, where the bilinear transform's frequency warping (≈ 0.05 % at 20 kHz
at 768 kHz, WDF_DESIGN §2) matters most; worst 0.019 dB against 0.3 dB allowed (0.078 dB at 384 kHz). Master scales v_oc(y) by a
frequency-independent ratio (the wiper is unloaded and VR2's total is fixed), so the dB error is the same
at every Master setting. Plot: `20261013_b4_tonestack_magnitude_error.png`.

### Knob jumps

| Case | ESR | Peak error / reference peak | Error energy > 5 kHz vs tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treble | −64.4 dB | 1.4e-3 | −68.4 dB |
| Middle | −64.3 dB | 1.2e-3 | −72.4 dB |
| Bass | −73.2 dB | 3.9e-4 | −109.9 dB |
| Master | −53.3 dB | 2.7e-3 | −56.1 dB |
| All four | −52.1 dB | 3.2e-3 | −54.8 dB |

The error comes from the rate-limited matrix updates (every 32 samples, a staircase in the pot values; at
768 kHz the steps are half as long, hence about 6 dB better than at 384 kHz): at 384 kHz with an update
every sample (a temporary build, not committed) the same cases gave −88 to −92 dB, which also shows that
the SPICE reference and the engine's smoothing agree.

### Cost (`amp2203_bench b4`)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Static knobs | 32.5 ns/sample (2.5 % of the 1.3 µs/sample budget at 768 kHz; 31.7 ns at 384 kHz) |
| One scattering-matrix update (12 ports, double-double, D-026) | ≈ 8.8 µs (reset(): update + DC solve) |
| Knobs moving continuously (update every 32 samples) | 277 ns/sample (≈ 21 % of the budget at 768 kHz, only while a knob moves) |

The update fits the audio thread, so there is no worker thread (WDF_DESIGN §5.4); kParamUpdateInterval
stays 32 (D-027).

### R_th4 for J4

The engine's `rth4` (D-015 formula from the smoothed knobs) matches the testbench formula as evaluated by
ngspice to 2e-16 relative at 11 settings.

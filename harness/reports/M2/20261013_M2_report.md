### Newton solver

The root equation is solved in K-port Thevenin form, `f(v) = v + Z·i(v) − v_oc` (D-028). The root ports
share k1a / k1b and Cgp, so Z has off-diagonal terms, and the diagonal form in the earlier WDF_DESIGN §4 did
not apply. With the plain warm start, 5 kHz 2 V failed (4213 failures, ESR −7.9 dB). V1b leaves cutoff at
6–7 V per sample, and there the plate current is exponential in Vgk (scale ≈ 0.46 V). The full step
overshoots, halving cuts it to 1/16, and 12 iterations are not enough. Linear extrapolation from the
two previous solutions as the initial guess (WDF_DESIGN §4 had listed it as a later optimisation that
needs harness evidence) gives 0 failures everywhere. D-029 has the measured variants.

| Preset | Median iterations (worst signal) | Max | Failures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Default (Volume 0.6) | 3 | 10 (5 kHz 2 V) | 0 |
| Volume 0.2 | 3 | 5 | 0 |
| Volume 1.0 | 3 | 12 (5 kHz 2 V, converged on the last allowed iteration) | 0 |

The Volume 1.0 maximum equals the limit, with no margin. B3 runs at higher levels (up to 50 V) and
will show whether more is needed (for example a better predictor or a junction-limiting step).

### Knob jump (Preamp Volume, SPEC §6.2, D-025)

Preamp Volume 0 → 1 in 10 ms during a 1 kHz 0.1 V tone. The engine is finite, with no Newton failures.
ESR vs SPICE with time-varying VR1 on the same smoothed trajectory is **−49.7 dB** (PASS, ≤ −40 dB), with
peak error 3.0e-3 of the reference peak.

## Open questions for the owner (Volume 0.2 FAILs)

**Q12 — STEP at Preamp Volume 0.2: ESR −34.5 dB (≤ −40 dB).** The error lies above 20 kHz. ESR is
−44.1 dB after a 40 kHz low-pass on both signals, −50.7 dB after 20 kHz and −63.7 dB after 5 kHz. With the
engine at 768 kHz and 1.536 MHz on the same input, ESR is −44.4 and −52.6 dB, so the deviation is the
bilinear discretisation at 384 kHz of an ideal step, whose spectrum reaches Nyquist. At Volume 0.2 the
C5 bright cap passes that content almost unattenuated while the low frequencies are cut. At Volumes
0.6 and 1.0 the same signal gives −52.1 and −63.2 dB.

**Q13 — Harmonics at Preamp Volume 0.2, 5 kHz 0.1 V: H3 off by 0.54 dB (≤ 0.5 dB).** H3 (15 kHz) is at
−70.7 dBc. The engine gives −71.23 dBc at 384, 768 and 1536 kHz. SPICE gives −71.28, −70.68, −71.03 and
−71.13 dBc at RELTOL 1e-5, 1e-6, 1e-7 and 1e-8, so the reference has not converged at 1e-6. At 1e-8 the
difference is 0.10 dB. SPICE time at 1e-8 is 2–3× that at 1e-6 (5 kHz sine 8 s → 25 s, sweep 58 s → 121 s).

## Open questions for the owner (the 3 FAILs)

**Q17 — Engine vs the SPICE chain at 5 kHz 0.5 V: −39.3 / −38.4 dB (≤ −40 dB, the "hot" level).**
This is the same discretisation effect as Q16, at a smaller margin. In the 384 kHz study the same case
went −33.1 / −39.4 / −44.2 dB at 1× / 2× / 4×, so 768 kHz lands just short of −40 dB. Newton converges
(max 9–12, 0 failures). At 2 V the overdrive limit (−30 dB) passes with −33.4 / −34.1 dB.

**Q18 — Partition at the loaded y, Volume 1.0, 5 kHz 2 V: −22.1 dB (≤ −26 dB).** The speaker output for the
same case is −27.3 dB. In the 384 kHz split of junctions this point was mostly J2 (J2 alone −24.7 dB):
at full Preamp Volume V1b is driven between cutoff and grid conduction, so its output impedance at p1b
swings far from the constant R_th2 = 89.7 kΩ. SPEC §4.1's upgrade path: (a) the reactive part of the
J2 source impedance (|Z| varies only 89.7 → 85.6 kΩ from 1 to 10 kHz, so it does not address a
large-signal change); (b) merge B12 and B34 under one 8-port root (no junction before the PI; larger
matrices and an 8×8 Newton).

## How M4 got here

| Step | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| EL34 model, Jacobian | 1464 + 5 SPICE points within 0.1 % / 1 nA; Jacobian within 1e−5 | D-040 |
| OT as one coupled element (no ideal transformer) | alone vs SPICE: AC at out / pA / pB within the linear limits and 1°, transient −123 dB at out | D-040 |
| Supply B7 | DC within 1 µV of SPICE; trapezoidal, inputs held one sample | D-040 |
| J5 (PI → power stage) | closed-loop SPICE study: −23 to −26 dB with \|Z_self\|, −46 to −68 dB with Z_self − Z_mutual, −14 to −21 dB at 20 V for both; 2×2 source not renderable (`20261013_partition_j5.md`) | Q19: merge B5 + B6 (D-041) |
| Power-stage Newton at OT flyback (Master / Presence 1.0, 1.9–2.2 V guitar peaks: up to 3157 failures in 30 ms) | step-limit / halving variants; SPICE shows the same plate swings (+1588 / −462 V) | free plate / screen steps, 2 halvings, source-stepping fallback (D-042) |

At the max settings above, with D-042: 77 / 12 first-solve failures in 30 ms (2.18 / 1.0 V input), all
rescued by the fallback (0 unrescued). The first solve's failure is still counted as a Newton failure;
rescued / unrescued are reported separately.

## Cost at 768 kHz (`amp2203_bench`, idle machine; real-time limit 1.3 µs per sample at 48 kHz × 16)

| Block | ns per sample |
|---|---|
| **Full amp** (60 s, 220 Hz, 0.1 FS, default knobs) | **2476 (real-time factor 0.5)** |
| Preamp B1234 | 1263–1857 |
| PowerAmp B56 (0.1–20 V at 1 kHz; 5 kHz 20 V) | 1583–2018; 3837 |
| PhaseInverter B5 / PowerStage B6 (separately) | 278–611 / 566–890 |

The merge costs 1.3–1.9× B5 + B6. The full amp is 1.9× slower than real time at 768 kHz and 19× above
the M5 target (real-time factor ≥ 10, 130 ns per sample). SPEC M5 asks to profile and report per-block costs
before optimising; the candidates are the M6 items (chord Newton, lookup tables for the device equations,
a cheaper knob-update path) and the internal rate.

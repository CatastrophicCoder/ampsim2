## Outcome in one paragraph

The plugin now runs the circuit model at 2x oversampling (96 kHz inside at 48 kHz) with the exact preamp → power-amp
coupling, the power amp at full rate and a Newton tolerance of 1e-4 V; it meets the −20 dB ESR target against SPICE
on every preset and knob test, including HOT. The original M6 goal (real-time headroom at 8x with the old
tolerances) was replaced by the owner: the tolerances became −20 dB (literature range, D-051) and the rate study
showed 2x is indistinguishable by ear from the 16x reference. The reference model gained a fitted EL34, a
representative speaker load, a physical plate-current clamp and C2. Against a NAM capture of a real 2203 at its
published settings the plugin sounded "pretty accurate" to the owner. CPU at high Master (≈ 47 % of one core) stays
above the 20 % working budget.

## Reference-model changes (owner-approved; CIRCUIT.md §5 / §6, reference/README.md updated)

| Decision | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-058 | EL34 Koren parameters fitted to 81 digitised Ei-RC curve points (median error 3.7 / 2.7 % vs 11.5 / 28.2 %) | owner listening ("dark", "lacks gain") led to an A5 review |
| D-059 | 16 Ω resistor → representative nominal 16 Ω 4x12 impedance (Re 13 Ω, 1.0 mH ‖ 45 Ω, 100 Hz resonance) | closed-loop output impedance ≈ 20 Ω: a real speaker load adds +3 … +5 dB at 100 Hz and in the treble |
| D-061 | EL34 plate voltage through a 5 V softplus: no plate current below the cathode | the reverse plate current in Koren's form stalled Newton during speaker-load flybacks at HOT |
| D-062 | C2 100 pF V1a plate–cathode (missing from the transcription, O8) | on the drawing |

Also fixed during M6: the standalone blocks' default supplies were still pre-D-058 (found by the D-062 block tests).
